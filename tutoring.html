Tutoring

One-on-one, conversation-led, built around what you're actually trying to understand rather than a fixed syllabus. Tap a subject for details.

  • Consumer and firm behavior, market structure, game theory basics. Problem-set heavy — we work through your actual coursework rather than abstract review.
  • Growth, inflation, monetary and fiscal policy, open-economy models. Good for connecting the models to what's actually in the news, which is usually where the confusion lives.
  • Asset allocation, risk-adjusted returns, and building a portfolio you can actually explain and defend. Aimed at students and career-switchers preparing for CFA-style material or their first real allocation decisions.
  • Calculus, linear algebra, and statistics — usually the math underlying an economics or finance course, taught alongside that course rather than in the abstract.
  • Conversation-led Czech for people living in or moving to Prague, from basics through the point where you stop translating in your head. Good for anyone who can order coffee but goes quiet in meetings.
  • Conversational and academic English, including essay writing and exam preparation. Lessons adjust to whatever you're actually using the language for — work, study, or travel.
  • Close reading of primary texts, argument reconstruction, and essay feedback. Good for exam prep, thesis chapters, or just working through a text that isn't clicking on your own.
  • From classical political economy through Keynes and the neoclassical synthesis to present debates. For understanding why economists disagree, not just what the models say.

Sessions run 60 or 90 minutes, online or in person in Prague. Rates above are for 60 minutes.
